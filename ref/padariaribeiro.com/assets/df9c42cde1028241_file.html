Our tradition
is just as it always was

The history of Padaria Ribeiro started to be written in 1878, in the old Praça do Pão, in the city of Porto. At the time, the founders instilled their business with the tradition and quality of products - traits that its successors have been able to keep alive until today. So many years later, Padaria Ribeiro continues to be a well-known name in Porto's patisserie and bakery market.
